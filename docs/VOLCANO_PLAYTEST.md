# Volcano expedition acceptance

This is a manual Studio / live-client checklist, not a claim of completed device testing. Start the integrated place with two clients where possible. Use fresh accounts or clean test data for one-time discoveries; never clear production data for this test.

## First expedition

1. From spawn, locate the volcano, climb part of its exterior, and find the tunnel without teleporting. Check collision, camera clearance, and whether landmarks are visible.
2. Watch one full 48-second breathing cycle. During the ten-second exhale, confirm luminous fissures and hidden marks change, the jaw moves subtly, and fire cultivation accelerates on the local source. No audio is bundled without a verified asset.
3. Cultivate on ember ground. Purify 40 mana for 10 advancement, then forge 40 fire into 30 solid fire. Verify the journal and ordinary advancement HUD agree. Repeat with insufficient resources and verify nothing is spent. Fill a vessel with non-fire mana and confirm purification still frees room.
4. Awaken the brazier for 10 fire. On a separate fresh character, Vent within 12 studs and line of sight: the same paid burst should awaken it without another charge. Confirm the ash seal disappears, the nearby recess becomes visible, and repeated activations do not create duplicate lights. Explore the refuge during an exhale and find the fissure. Verify each discovery pays once, including after leaving and reconnecting.
5. Speak to the dragon without enough solid fire, then with 30. Verify the first interaction gives a clue and the latter consumes exactly one offering. Repeated conversations must not charge again.
6. Begin at the lower trial stone. Traverse on foot to the upper stone in 6–90 seconds. Confirm Cinder Step unlocks. Repeat after dying, respawning, or exceeding 90 seconds: these attempts must fail until restarted.
7. Use Q / the Step button facing a clear route, then facing walls and low obstructions. Verify 25 fire and a six-second cooldown, no movement through solid geometry, and no cost for a blocked route. A jump over an open drop remains a real fall risk.
8. Use V / the Vent button near another eligible combatant. Verify modest short-range damage respects normal engagement rules. Check dead, seated, stunned, and casting restrictions.
9. Reconnect and verify solid fire, discoveries, dragon offering, and Cinder Step remain. Fire follows the existing vessel persistence rules.
10. At the optional crater vent rings, observe rising warning sparks before the active column. Remain on a pad during the active exhale and verify bounded heat damage; step off and verify it stops. The source on the opposite side remains reachable without crossing a vent. Check spawn protection and death handling.

## Multiplayer and interface

- Two players can receive their own discovery rewards; one player's lit brazier is visible to everyone. One player's completion must not unlock another player.
- Spoofed station action names through the remote do nothing. Only server ProximityPrompt callbacks can forge, purify, claim, offer, or complete a trial. Test repeated triggering and distant prompt attempts.
- Inspect desktop, narrow viewport, and a real touch device: the journal occupies the right middle edge, avoiding the left Sense panel and upper-right player list, and starts collapsed on touch. Its +/− button must work. Confirm Q/V countdowns follow server cooldowns, buttons remain reachable and text stays legible. Desktop resizing alone is not physical-device evidence.
- Ask an unfamiliar player to find the dragon and a secret without narration. Record confusion, navigation dead ends, collision snags, and whether they choose a second visit.

## Scope limits

The dragon offers a local solid-fire exchange, not a general NPC shop or player marketplace. Vent is a coarse combat action and can awaken the brazier. The traversal trial checks server location, character continuity and elapsed time; it is not a general movement anticheat. Thermal hazards occupy optional marked pads with warning particles and safe alternatives. No audio has been added without a verified sound asset. Saved unlocks are implemented; runtime acceptance must be recorded separately from automated rule tests and successful builds.
