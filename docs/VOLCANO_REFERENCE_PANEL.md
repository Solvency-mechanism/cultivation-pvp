# Refinement reference panel

Sources checked 2026-09-12. These are selected comparison references, not evidence that our scene has surpassed them. No source assets are copied into the game.

- [Beyond the Dark](https://create.roblox.com/docs/resources/beyond-the-dark): official Roblox high-fidelity showcase and documented modular architecture / character / sound workflow. Reference purpose: coherent materials, controlled light and production discipline.
- [FRONTLINES](https://www.roblox.com/games/5938036553/FRONTLINES): reference candidate for close-view surface detail and readability under motion. Current official listing verified; controlled gameplay captures and comparative evaluation remain pending.
- [The Mimic](https://www.roblox.com/games/6243699076/The-Mimic): reference candidate for environmental reveal and atmosphere. Current official listing verified; controlled gameplay captures and comparative evaluation remain pending.

Do not compare promotional thumbnails to our gameplay screenshots. The later review needs actual gameplay under named settings, comparable viewing distance and exposure. We have not performed the three-reviewer comparison or physical-device acceptance.

Current asset policy: the implemented pass uses authored native geometry, built-in Roblox materials and bundled particle/audio paths. No third-party asset upload, imported texture or custom sculpted mesh is present yet. The bespoke mesh / texture stage remains a quality gap against the full sprint ambition.
